# Design Request DR-001: thermometer separation and settings menu

Status: answered and consolidated on 2026-08-14

The existing driving gauge must not be redesigned. Specify only the revised
temperature icon and the new settings surface below, then consolidate the accepted
result into `docs/BUILD-SPEC.md`, `docs/UI_DESIGN.md`, the editable reference, and
the firmware interaction specification.

## Already decided by Marcos

- Open settings by pressing and holding the gauge for 700 ms.
- The menu includes brightness, warning-sound volume, and demo/normal data-source
  selection.
- The temperature icon receives a taller thermometer stem and three raised
  horizontal marks; none may intersect or visually touch the oil waves.
- The thermometer stem must remain visibly taller than the three marks: its top
  painted edge sits above the top mark's painted edge.
- The main 50/50 driving screen otherwise remains unchanged.
- `SENSORES`/normal mode remains visibly locked as `CALIBRACIÓN PENDIENTE` until
  ADS1115 and AC-13 through AC-18 have real passing evidence.

## Approved v1

### Temperature icon geometry

- Preserve the current silhouette, color behavior, width, bulb, and two oil-wave
  rows.
- Extend the stem upward by 3 px while retaining its current lower endpoint.
- Place the three native side marks at y = 3, 15, and 27 px (currently 9, 22,
  and 34 px), preserving their size and horizontal position.
- Keep the stem's top painted edge at least 3 px above the top mark's painted
  edge.
- Require at least a 6 px clear gap between the third mark's painted bounds and the
  upper wave's painted bounds in both the editable SVG and native LVGL geometry.

### Settings hierarchy

1. `PANTALLA`
   - `BRILLO` slider, 5–100%, live preview, persisted.
2. `SONIDO WARNING`
   - `ACTIVADO` switch.
   - `VOLUMEN` slider, 5–100%, persisted.
   - `PROBAR SONIDO` button using the real double beep.
3. `FUENTE DE DATOS`
   - `DEMO` available.
   - `SENSORES` visible but disabled with `CALIBRACIÓN PENDIENTE` until the safety
     gate passes; choosing it before then changes nothing.
4. `ACCESIBILIDAD`
   - `PARPADEO WARNING`: current elements at `2 HZ`, full screen at `2 HZ`, or
     `FIJO`; `FIJO` keeps the warning solid red.
   - In `PANTALLA 2 HZ`, alternate an opaque full-screen red field and the normal
     black gauge every 250 ms. During the red phase, redraw the white pressure
     number above the field at its normal position; the number is never hidden.
5. `SISTEMA`
   - `DIAGNÓSTICO`: read-only firmware version, current source, FPS, display/touch,
     audio, and later ADS1115 status.
   - `RESTABLECER AJUSTES`, protected by a confirmation screen.

### Interaction proposal

- Set LVGL's pointer long-press time to exactly 700 ms; scrolling or dragging does
  not open the menu.
- Open as a full-screen black settings page so every control has a large touch
  target and remains readable in the car.
- `VOLVER` returns to the gauge. Ten seconds without interaction also returns and
  saves any changed setting.
- A pressure warning interrupts/closes the menu immediately and restores the gauge
  warning; settings never hide an active alarm.
- Persist brightness, sound enabled/volume, and warning-motion preference in NVS.
  Data-source mode persists only after the sensor safety gate exists; until then
  every boot remains in demo mode.
- If NVS is missing/corrupt, use the current compile-time defaults and show no
  fabricated sensor state.

### Units

- Include `UNIDADES`: PSI/bar.
- Changing units converts displayed values, thresholds, and labels from one
  canonical pressure value; it must not alter the underlying measurement or
  calibration.

## Deferred or rejected additions

- Defer manual `DÍA`/`NOCHE` presets. Investigate automatic ambient-light sensing
  first; keep the manual brightness slider in v1.
- Touch lock requiring a second 700 ms hold to prevent accidental menu entry.
- Display orientation; not recommended for the fixed 52 mm enclosure design.
- Editable alarm thresholds; not recommended before vehicle calibration and safety
  validation.

## Answers recorded from Marcos on 2026-08-14

1. Approved the proposed v1 hierarchy and interaction behavior.
2. Include PSI/bar units now.
3. Defer manual `DÍA`/`NOCHE` presets while automatic light detection is researched.
4. App `002581d` warning appearance is clean; the dotted artifact is gone.
5. Add a selectable full-screen 2 Hz warning mode.
6. The pressure number must never be hidden, including either phase of the
   full-screen warning.

## Final full-screen warning contract

`PANTALLA 2 HZ` alternates an opaque full-screen red field for 250 ms and the normal
black gauge for 250 ms. The red phase may cover every other gauge element, but it
must redraw the pressure number in white above the field at the same centered
position. The normal phase uses the ordinary gauge number. The pressure value is
therefore continuously visible and stable through both phases. A literal blackout
that hides the number is rejected.
